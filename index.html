What happens if I leave my route?
  1. The guardian bot calls you and asks if you are safe.
  2. If you are safe, you say why you chose this place. It is marked on your map.
  3. Then the bot checks your face before the trip continues.
  4. If you miss 3 calls, the nearest police station and your family get an alert with your location.
  5. If you say you are not safe, police and family are alerted and the camera opens. It closes only when it sees your face and hears “I am safe now”.
